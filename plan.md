## Pain points of the customer to address.

This must be a summarized, or maybe they can be like title that are collapsed and you can expand.

Main issue:
- Wasting your night, time, money and holiday and not finding that experience you where looking for.
- You want to spend less time sitting and more time dancing good.

Problems?
1. Not being known in the scene: Feel part of the dance scene
- Not having dance invitations
- Not dancing in the whole night or dancing really little
- Feeling out of the party
- You want to have someone to guide you though the experience

2. Quality of the dancers: Dance at the level you want
- You don't get to enjoy enough with the people that invites you.
- You feel like they are not at your level
- You want to level up your dance in milongas but the people that invites you are not up to your standards.
- You want a partner who matches your rhythm and skill level.

3. On Classes: Get more from classes
- Issues finding partner for a class
- You need someone that can translate a class while taking it with him/her.

4. Socializing: Connect with the local community
- You want to meet locals but find it difficult, a local dancer can help you integrate.
- You feel a bit nervous going alone to milongas.

5. Practice: Practice in a real setting
- You’d like to practice outside your regular lessons.
- You struggle to remember techniques in a live setting, someone that you feel confortable to ask in the middle of the tanda can be helpful.
- You want to try a new things without feeling judged.
- You want more confidence before a party, wedding, or competition.

6. Confort: Enjoy comfort and peace of mind
- You’d like reliable, respectful company for the entire event.
- You want personalized guidance while still having fun.
- You want to feel safe and comfortable on the dance floor.
- You’re traveling and want a trusted local dance partner for your limited time.
